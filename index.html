What we do

Farm chores, house pets, and everything in between

Book us for one thing or all of it.

Our specialty
🐐

Farm & livestock sitting

Morning and evening chores, done the way you do them.

  • Feeding & watering, all species
  • Chickens: feed, water, collect, lock up at dusk
  • Goats & sheep: hay, grain, hoof checks
  • Horses: stalls, turnout, blanketing
  • Pigs, donkeys, cattle & barn cats
  • Medications, injections, wound care
🐕

Pet sitting & dog walking

Drop-in visits, overnights, and walks for the animals that live inside.

  • 30-min drop-ins or overnights
  • Feeding, litter, meds, playtime
  • Puppies, seniors, and anxious dogs
🏡

House sitting

Your place looks lived-in while you're gone.

  • Mail, packages, trash to the road
  • Plants, gardens, lights on timers
  • Frozen-pipe and storm checks
🧸

Babysitting

Evenings, weekends, and date nights.

  • Both of us CPR certified
  • Experience with ages 1–12
  • Parent references available
💪

Happy to help with more

If you need a hand with something else while we're out there, just ask.

  • Stacking hay, mucking stalls
  • Walking fence lines
  • Moving help & heavy lifting
